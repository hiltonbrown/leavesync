# Team Calendar go-to-market plan

> **Reconciled 24 August 2026**: The 21 August instrumentation milestone was not
> met. Current source proves Clerk lifecycle instrumentation, but not the full
> application-to-activation event set in this plan. The public site still uses
> direct sign-up rather than a closed-cohort application flow. Treat both as
> launch blockers, not completed assumptions. Pricing remains A$19/A$49 and the
> launch remains Australia-only.

## Strategic recommendation

Launch on **14 September 2026** as an Australia-only expansion of closed early
access, not as full general availability.

Initial offer:

> Team Calendar is the Xero-native leave and availability layer for small
> Australian teams. Staff request leave once, managers approve with coverage in
> view, approved leave writes back to Xero, and payroll leave, WFH, travel and
> contractor availability appear in the calendars the team already uses.

The initial cohort should contain 8 to 12 Australian businesses with 8 to 30
people. Prioritise calendar-led teams, not shift-based workforces.

This matches the current product positioning and closed-AU launch mode. NZ and
UK promotion should wait until those regional adapters are fully implemented
and verified.

| Objective | Nine-week target | Measurement |

| Calendar adoption | 30 active feed tokens observed by feed-access telemetry | Feed access analytics |

| Advocacy | 3 case studies, 5 App Store reviews when listed | Published assets |

| Support | First response within four business hours | Support records |

An organisation counts as activated only when it has:

1. Connected an Australian Xero Payroll organisation.
2. Completed its initial employee and leave sync.
3. Created at least one secure calendar feed.
4. Subscribed that feed to a calendar client.
5. Completed one leave submission and manager approval that writes back to
   Xero.

### Milestones

- **28 August (recovery gate):** Reconcile positioning and public claims; finish
  application, signup, Xero connection, sync, feed, approval and activation
  instrumentation that was due 21 August.
- **28 August:** Three reference customers and ten adviser prospects secured.

- **25 September:** Eight organisations onboarded or scheduled.

### Launch gates

Do not launch unless:

- All public pages consistently say Australian closed early access.
- Pricing, seat limits and payroll-organisation limits agree across the website
  and product.
- Acquisition through activation is instrumented.
- Three reference customers have completed the core workflow.
- There are no unresolved critical incidents.
- Support coverage and rollback procedures are confirmed.
- Claims distinguish Team Calendar publishing speed from calendar-client
  refresh timing.

## 2. Target audience

### Primary segments

1. **Professional services firms with 8 to 30 people**

   Accounting practices, agencies, consultancies, legal firms, architecture
   practices and IT services.

2. **Hybrid and mixed-workforce businesses**

   Teams containing employees, contractors, directors or advisers whose
   combined availability affects client delivery.

3. **Small allied-health practices**

   Clinics where cross-team availability matters, but shift rostering is not
   the main requirement.

4. **Xero accountants, bookkeepers and cloud integrators**

   These are referral partners rather than primary product users. Xero actively
   encourages accountants and bookkeepers to recommend useful applications to
   clients, and its Australian partner network is a significant distribution
   channel. [Xero's networking
   guidance](https://www.xero.com/au/accountant-bookkeeper-guides/business-networking/)

Deprioritise:

- Businesses with fewer than five people.
- Hospitality, retail and other roster-led teams.
- Enterprises seeking a complete HRIS.
- Buyers primarily seeking time clocks, award interpretation or workforce
  scheduling.

### User personas

| Persona | Role | Main problem | Core message |
|---|---|---|---|
| Owner or managing director | Economic buyer | Wants fewer payroll errors and coverage surprises without buying a full HR suite | “A focused availability layer for Xero, not another HR platform.” |
| Operations, office or payroll manager | Champion and administrator | Re-enters leave and maintains shared calendars | “One request, one approval, Xero and every calendar stay aligned.” |
| Team or practice manager | Frequent user | Needs to assess cover before approving leave | “See who else is away before you approve.” |
| Employee or contractor | Adoption user | Wants an easy request process and clear status | “Request once and track it clearly.” |
| Xero adviser | Referrer | Wants dependable, low-admin applications for clients | “Keep Xero authoritative while removing duplicate leave administration.” |

### Where they are

- Xero adviser directory and App Store.
- LinkedIn, especially owner, operations, payroll and practice-management
  audiences.
- Xero-focused accountant and bookkeeper networks.
- AHRI and Australian Payroll Association events and webinars.
- XU Hub and other Xero ecosystem publications.
- Local chambers and professional-services networks.
- Relevant Xero and Australian small-business communities.

The Xero App Store should become the most important long-term
owned-distribution channel. Current Xero guidance says certification requires
at least three customers and gives listed partners access to App Store
visibility, customer stories, webinars and other ecosystem opportunities.
[Xero App Store partner
guide](https://developer.xero.com/static/otherfiles/Grow-with-the-Xero-App-Store.pdf)

## 3. Launch channels

### Owned channels

| Channel | Tactics | Cadence | KPI |
|---|---|---|---|
| Website | AU-specific landing page, interactive workflow demonstration, security page, clear “Apply for early access” CTA | Complete by week -2 | 5% visitor-to-application rate |
| Email | Prospect, applicant, onboarding and adviser sequences | 1 to 2 sends weekly | 35% open, 5% click rate |
| Blog | Problem education, workflow explanation, security and customer proof | One article weekly | Qualified organic visits and assisted applications |
| Product onboarding | Guided checklist from Xero connection to first calendar subscription | Per organisation | 70% seven-day activation |
| Help centre | Xero setup, privacy, calendar subscription and troubleshooting guides | Ready before launch | Reduced support volume |
| Founder profile | Product demonstrations and operational insights | Three posts weekly | Qualified conversations |

Replace the current direct public signup with an application flow before calling
access closed. Capture company size, country, Xero Payroll usage, calendar
client, current process and acquisition source. Until that ships, the
"Apply for guided early access" CTA and demand-generation KPI are not measurable
as written.

### Paid channels

| Channel | Tactic | Budget | Rule |
|---|---|---:|---|
| Google Search | Exact and phrase match for “Xero leave calendar”, “Xero leave management”, “Xero leave Outlook” and close variants | A$3,500 | Highest priority |
| LinkedIn | Retarget site visitors and matched adviser/prospect lists | A$1,500 | Avoid broad cold campaigns |
| Association media | One payroll or HR webinar/newsletter test | A$2,500 | Require audience and lead reporting |
| Adviser or creator collaboration | Sponsored workflow demonstration or webinar | A$1,500 | Use payroll/accounting specialists only |

LinkedIn should remain a small retargeting channel because current B2B CPCs are
high relative to Team Calendar's A$19/A$49 monthly plans. [LinkedIn advertising
benchmark](https://searchengineland.com/linkedin-ads-cpc-benchmarks-how-costs-compare-with-google-ads-481010)

Paid-media stop rules:

- Pause an ad group after 40 clicks without a qualified application.
- Pause any channel that spends A$1,000 without producing a qualified
  opportunity.
- Target no more than A$150 per qualified application.
- Target no more than A$300 per activated organisation.
- Do not scale unless projected 12-month gross-margin payback remains
  acceptable.

### Earned and partnership channels

- Recruit 10 Xero accounting or bookkeeping practices.
- Provide each partner with:
  - A two-minute demo.
  - A one-page client-fit checklist.
  - A guided-onboarding offer.
  - A tracked referral link.
  - Co-branded email copy.
- Pitch XU Hub and payroll publications with an educational angle: “Why
  approved leave still fails to reach the team calendar”.
- Co-host a launch webinar with a credible Xero adviser.
- Ask activated customers for non-incentivised App Store reviews after their
  second successful sync.
- Attend local chamber and Xero ecosystem events, but defer conference booths.
- Approach the Australian Payroll Association and AHRI for contributed content
  or a webinar before considering larger sponsorships.

### Community channels

- Publish three founder-led LinkedIn posts each week:
  - One operational problem.
  - One product workflow demonstration.
  - One customer or pilot result.
- Add 10 useful comments or answers weekly in relevant accounting, payroll and
  operations discussions.
- Participate transparently in Xero-adjacent communities. Disclose affiliation
  and only link when directly useful.
- Use Product Hunt as a credibility asset, not a primary acquisition forecast.
- Avoid generic startup and broad small-business communities unless the post
  solves a specific Xero workflow problem.

## 5. Content strategy

### Launch announcement

Headline:

> One leave request. Xero and your team calendars stay aligned.

Supporting message:

> Team Calendar brings leave, working from home and everyday availability into
> one workflow for small Australian businesses using Xero Payroll.

Primary CTA: **Apply for guided early access**

The announcement should demonstrate:

1. Employee submits leave.
2. Manager sees team coverage and approves.
3. Approved leave writes back to Xero.
4. Availability publishes to Outlook, Google Calendar or Apple Calendar.
5. Xero remains authoritative for balances and accruals.

### Blog schedule

| Timing | Article |
|---|---|
| Week -3 | “How many places does one leave request touch?” |
| Week -1 | “What Team Calendar changes, and what stays in Xero” |
| Launch day | “Introducing Team Calendar for Australian Xero Payroll teams” |
| Week +1 | “Secure calendar feeds: privacy, revocation and visibility” |
| Week +2 | Customer case study with quantified admin reduction |
| Week +3 | “Leave calendar or full HRIS: which does a small team need?” |
| Week +4 | Launch findings and product roadmap |

Avoid unverified superlatives and absolute reliability claims. Team Calendar
can state that it republishes feeds promptly, but calendar-client display timing
varies.

### Social content

Post three times per week:

- Monday: Pain or workflow insight.
- Wednesday: Short screen recording or annotated product demonstration.
- Friday: Customer result, build note or objection response.

Content themes:

- Duplicate leave entry.
- Checking cover before approval.
- Xero as source of truth.
- Mixed employee and contractor availability.
- Privacy-controlled feeds.
- Why Team Calendar is not a payroll engine or full HRIS.

### Email campaigns

#### Prospect sequence

1. Week -2: “How many places do you update when someone takes leave?”
2. Week -1: “A simpler leave workflow for Xero Payroll teams”
3. Launch day: “Team Calendar early access is open”
4. Day +3: Customer workflow and demonstration.
5. Day +10: Security, privacy and Xero source-of-truth explanation.
6. Day +21: Final cohort invitation.

#### Adviser sequence

1. Client-fit checklist.
2. Two-minute demonstration.
3. Webinar invitation.
4. Referral follow-up with guided-onboarding offer.

#### Onboarding sequence

1. Connect Xero.
2. Review people and leave types.
3. Create the first feed.
4. Subscribe it to a calendar.
5. Complete the first approval.
6. Book a 14-day review.

## 6. Budget allocation

Recommended nine-week cash budget: **A$15,000**, excluding salaries, GST and
major travel.

| Allocation | Budget | Share |
|---|---:|---:|
| High-intent Google Search | A$3,500 | 23% |
| LinkedIn retargeting | A$1,500 | 10% |
| Association webinar or newsletter | A$2,500 | 17% |
| Adviser and specialist creator collaborations | A$1,500 | 10% |
| Demo video, case study and campaign creative | A$2,500 | 17% |
| Xero partner tier and campaign tools | A$1,000 | 7% |
| Local events and travel | A$500 | 3% |
| Contingency | A$2,000 | 13% |

Release spending in stages:

- **40% pre-launch:** Only after instrumentation and three reference customers
  are confirmed.
- **35% launch week:** Only after the readiness gate passes.
- **25% post-launch:** Only for channels producing qualified applications and
  activated organisations.

### Resource requirements

- Founder or GM: Two days per week for sales, advisers, webinars and demos.
- GTM generalist: Three days per week.
- Product/customer-success lead: One day per week, two during launch week.
- Designer/content contractor: One to two days per week.
- Engineer or analytics owner: One day per week during pre-launch, then launch
  on-call.
- Three reference customers and ten adviser partners.

### Weekly operating cadence

- Monday: Funnel and campaign review.
- Wednesday: Customer feedback and activation review.
- Friday: Spend, reliability and support review.
- Maintain one dashboard covering source, application, qualification,
  activation, product adoption, paid intent and support load.
- Scale a channel only after it produces at least three activated organisations
  or a credible partner-driven equivalent.

The strongest expected acquisition loop is:

**Xero adviser introduction → guided pilot → first successful approval and
calendar subscription → case study and App Store review → next adviser
referral.**
